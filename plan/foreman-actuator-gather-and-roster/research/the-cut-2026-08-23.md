## Mechanics, and the one thing this cut could NOT do

Membership was moved by **parent-child edge only**. No dependency edge was used for
thread membership: that is the documented permanently-undispatchable trap, and it fails
closed whenever the sibling repo is absent from `cross_repo_targets`.

**Dotted ids could not be freed.** `plan_child_ids_from_id_hierarchy` — read from the
plugin source at
`.../commands/_plan_child_edges.py`, not inferred — matches any id starting with
`overseer-tdfe.`, so 25 re-parented rows remain gate children of `overseer-tdfe`
whatever their parent says. They are now bound to **two** archive gates.

That is `overseer-tdfe.9`'s defect exactly: *"re-parenting frees a non-dotted child from
its epic's gate but a dotted child stays bound, so escape depends on id shape rather
than disposition."* It is retained under this thread and is now the **archive-blocking
prerequisite for four other threads**, which is a considerably stronger reason to fix it
than it had this morning.

**The alternative was rejected deliberately.** The established workaround is to file a
fresh-id successor and close the original as superseded — which is how
`overseer-tdfe.11`, `.2` through `.6` and `.13` through `.19` came to exist. Doing that
for 25 rows would have churned every id, orphaned their comment histories, and traded a
known gate defect for a large records-hygiene defect. The parent edges are what a human
and the roster read; the id-hierarchy binding is a gate artifact with an owner.
