## Proposal: Shell-only busy sessions remain eligible for guarded low-context wrap-up

### Target specification files

- SPECIFICATION/spec.md
- SPECIFICATION/contracts.md
- SPECIFICATION/constraints.md
- SPECIFICATION/scenarios.md

### Summary

Narrow unbounded action suppression from every busy classification to active generation, pane change, sub-agent activity, and unresolved ambiguity. A low-context session whose only busy evidence is a background shell may receive the existing escalating wrap-up when its runtime-specific eligible-input evidence is present and settled; restart authorization and every other interlock remain unchanged.

### Motivation

Supervisor sessions continuously carry a monitoring shell as their steady state. The ratified any-busy suppression therefore prevents them, and equally situated workers, from ever receiving the marker protocol's low-context wrap-up. This contradicts full supervisor-pair citizenship and the maintainer's 2026-08-02 ruling that the monitoring shell must not prevent a low-context restart instruction. The existing pair keep-going nudge already establishes that an atomic paste at a positively verified empty and settled prompt is safe when background-shell evidence is the only busy evidence.

### Proposed Changes

1. In spec.md's pane-evidence paragraph and sections ‘The supervision round’ and ‘The escalating wrap-up’, state that a round MAY open and a wrap-up MAY be pasted at or below the threshold only when the runtime-specific eligible-input predicate holds and two captures prove the pane settled. For Claude, eligible input means the input box is positively verified empty. For Codex, which cannot distinguish its empty rotating placeholder from typed text, eligible input means its existing structural idle-input evidence: a live Codex prompt and statusline, with no generating marker or picker; this evidence divergence applies to the low-context wrap-up only and MUST NOT weaken the pair nudge's stronger positively-empty requirement. The pane MUST NOT be generating or changing, MUST NOT carry sub-agent-busy evidence, MUST NOT show a structured gate or runtime-reported human wait, MUST NOT carry `blocked:` or `ready`, and MUST NOT carry a fresh winding-down acknowledgement. A stale winding-down acknowledgement MAY resume escalation. Background-shell evidence alone MUST NOT prevent the wrap-up when all of those guards hold; the daemon MUST leave the shell running and the wrap-up remains information for the session, never authorization to terminate work or restart. Replace the over-broad sentence that every false busy positive merely suppresses action with this evidence-kind split.

2. In spec.md section ‘The keep-going nudge’, replace the false count that calls the pair nudge the ONE bounded exception to busy classification suppressing acts. Define the shared safety floor instead: an informational paste MUST remain suppressed while the session is generating, changing, gated, runtime-reported as waiting on a human, sub-agent-busy, blocked by declaration, or the act's own runtime-specific input predicate is not satisfied; background-shell evidence alone does not suppress an otherwise qualified atomic informational paste. Enumerate the two qualified applications under the ratified contract: the low-context wrap-up for any supervised entity, using the runtime divergence above, and the bounded pair-stall nudge into the supervisor, retaining its stronger positively-empty requirement. Their independent threshold, episode, declaration, and round guards remain unchanged.

3. In spec.md section ‘Fail-soft posture’, narrow the busy-overfire claim. Ambiguous evidence MUST still resolve toward no act, and generating/changing/sub-agent-busy panes MUST retain unbounded action suppression. Shell-only eligibility MUST be affirmative and runtime-specific: recognized Claude registry `status=shell`, or the Codex descendant-shell fallback; an unknown, conflicting, malformed, or unavailable authoritative status MUST suppress the paste. A busy classification supported solely by recognized background-shell evidence MUST NOT suppress a low-context wrap-up once the runtime-specific input and settle guards establish a safe paste point. The report-only winddown-starved and shell-prolonged attention members MUST remain, but their ‘surfacing is the entire response’ wording MUST be scoped to what those attention conditions authorize: they authorize no paste, kill, declaration write, or restart. That report-only rule MUST NOT prohibit the separately qualified threshold wrap-up.

4. In contracts.md section ‘The wrap-up injection’, replace ‘verified idle-input pane’ with the explicit acting predicate: effective context at or below threshold; the runtime-specific eligible-input evidence defined above; two-capture settlement; no generation, pane change, sub-agent-busy evidence, structured gate, runtime-reported human wait, `blocked:` or `ready` declaration, or fresh winding-down acknowledgement. Both settle captures MUST retain the eligible input shape and be identical. Immediately before paste, the daemon MUST re-read managed-pane identity, authoritative runtime busy evidence, declaration, gate, and eligible-input state; any changed, conflicting, unreadable, or unknown signal cancels the act for that tick. The contract MUST state that no-busy and affirmatively shell-only-busy panes can satisfy this predicate, while any other busy evidence cannot. A certifiable `ready` takes the restart path only after every existing restart guard passes; an uncertifiable `ready` remains report-only and is not pasted into; malformed state remains fail-closed as no declaration. The existing stamp-before-paste, durable bands, atomic paste, runtime-aware submission, and ready-only restart interlock MUST remain unchanged.

5. In constraints.md section ‘Acting safety’, replace the absolute claim that every busy pane is never pasted into with the same evidence-kind and act-kind split. A low-context informational wrap-up MAY be pasted into an affirmatively shell-only-busy pane only under the complete predicate above. Generating, changing, sub-agent-busy, gated, human-waiting, foreign, bare-shell, and ambiguous panes MUST never be pasted into. Restart remains stricter: even a fresh certifiable `ready` MUST NOT restart while any busy evidence, including a background shell, remains; the session must stop its shell before restart. Atomic paste and same-runtime restart guarantees remain unchanged.

6. In scenarios.md, add separate canonical cases for: (a) Claude `status=shell` and (b) Codex descendant-shell fallback at or below threshold satisfying their runtime-specific input and settle predicates — each writes the stamp, pastes exactly one wrap-up, leaves the shell running, and performs no restart without a later fresh session-written `ready`; (c) generating and sub-agent-busy evidence suppressing paste; (d) pane change or evidence change during settlement suppressing paste; and (e) gates, human waits, `blocked:`, `ready`, fresh acknowledgement, and unknown/conflicting runtime evidence suppressing paste. Co-edit the danger scenario: danger membership authorizes reporting only and never restart, kill, or declaration writes, but does not suppress an independently qualified escalation-band wrap-up. Add a danger-band shell-only case asserting both the wrap-up and the report with no restart absent fresh `ready`. The implementation MUST register every new scenario heading in tests/heading-coverage.json atomically with deterministic tests.

7. On ratification, the realization sweep MUST align .claude-plugin/prose/overseer.md, overseer/marker-protocol.md, and any module docstrings or comments that currently say a false busy always suppresses injection. This sweep MUST preserve the cardinal rule, no-auto-spawn rule, no-force-kill rule, danger report-only behavior, coalescing, durable stamps, acknowledgement handling, and background-shell attention floors.
