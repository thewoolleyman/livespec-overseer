## Decision and Rationale

Accepted after two INDEPENDENT ratification reviews of these exact bytes, both returning NO BLOCKERS. The change closes a silent mutual stall observed live 2026-08-19 and since reproduced mechanically: every existing pane predicate returns identical values for a picker with an unconsumed inbound delivery behind it and for an ordinary picker, so the governed surface could not distinguish them. Round 1 returned two blockers, both real and both fixed before acceptance: the picker-authoring floor was a bare MUST with no scenario, and both floors keyed on a row field that the governed row enumeration did not carry, which would have left a required guarantee resting on an ungoverned field. Round 2 independently confirmed placement, distinctness from the age-keyed picker-stall projection and from escalation-exhausted, terminology parity with sibling members, and that picker_open collides with neither the snapshot's OBSERVATION-ONLY clause nor its note-elision rule. The cardinal rule is restated verbatim in the new floor and is unaffected; the membership authorizes no act; no timer- or heuristic-driven restart path is added.
