## Proposal: Retarget the ready-void mechanism to max-age expiry across spec.md, scenarios.md, and contracts.md (supersedes and replaces proposed_changes/supersede-void-notice-with-expiry-notice.md)

### Target specification files

- SPECIFICATION/spec.md
- SPECIFICATION/scenarios.md
- SPECIFICATION/contracts.md

### Summary

Full revision of the earlier same-topic proposal (SPECIFICATION/proposed_changes/supersede-void-notice-with-expiry-notice.md), which an independent adversarial review returned NOT READY against with four blocking findings. This revision: (1) adds contracts.md as a target and retargets its entire ready-void mechanism -- the ordered two-step death (record floor, then delete file), the certification-floor and non-monotonic-clock defense, the round-sidecar VOID INSTANT field, and the two void-grounded attention members -- onto the max-age-expiry trigger instead of deleting or leaving it stale; (2) sweeps every other spec.md location that referenced activity-voiding (204-207, 215, 233, 309-312, 378, 784-792) so no internal contradiction remains; (3) states explicitly that expiry DELETES the state file and RAISES the certification floor, mirroring the old void's two-step ordering and its crash-safety rationale exactly -- an expired-but-undeleted declaration would otherwise both remain capable of certifying a stale restart and make its own notice undeliverable under the unchanged guarded-paste predicate; (4) retargets all nine scenarios.md entries that ratified the activity-void trigger or its notice, including the five the first draft missed, and retargets rather than retires the repeated-voiding scenario since a round can still accumulate multiple expiries; (5) names the 30-minute default explicitly and states its overridability; (6) explicitly severs the `blocked:` void, which is UNCHANGED and orthogonal to this supersession -- only the `ready`-side voiding is retired.

### Motivation

Epic overseer-xkrwm3 (resume-submit-integrity), fronts 4-6, maintainer-directed 2026-08-16, ruling relayed the same day: work-item overseer-xawpyl's arm-until-idle redesign (PR #1001) retires activity-based ready-voiding to fix a confabulation trap, and the daemon's obligation to notify the session when its declaration dies transfers from the retired activity-void trigger to the new max-age-expiry trigger. The first attempt at this supersession (proposed_changes/supersede-void-notice-with-expiry-notice.md) covered only the void-notice paragraph and three scenarios, and was returned NOT READY by an independently-spawned Fable-model adversarial review with four blocking findings: contracts.md was not targeted at all despite holding the actual voiding mechanism as ratified normative text (the ordered two-step death, the certification-floor non-monotonic-clock defense, the sidecar VOID INSTANT field, two attention members grounded in voiding); spec.md itself retained internal contradictions at four other locations plus a dangling cross-reference; the replacement text never stated whether expiry deletes the state file or raises the certification floor, and as drafted this made the notice undeliverable under the unchanged guarded-paste predicate (which refuses to paste into any pane still showing `ready`) AND weakened the stale-certification safety guarantee (an expired-but-present declaration would still pass the interlock's precondition 3 the moment a pane looked idle); and the scenario sweep found only 2 of at least 7 additional scenarios needing retargeting. This proposal supersedes the incomplete first attempt with a comprehensive revision addressing all four blocking findings plus its two should-fix findings (retarget rather than retire the repeated-voiding scenario; name the 30-minute constant), so master never holds a self-contradictory or safety-weakening spec tree once PR #1001 (to be amended separately to match) merges.

### Proposed Changes

GENERAL RENAME, applied throughout spec.md, scenarios.md, and contracts.md wherever the text below does not give more specific replacement wording: `void`/`voided`/`voiding` (in the READY-declaration sense only -- NOT the separate, unchanged `blocked:` void) becomes `expire`/`expired`/`expiry`; `void instant` becomes `expiry instant`; `void-notice` becomes `expiry-notice`; `VOID INSTANT` (the sidecar field name) becomes `EXPIRY INSTANT`. The `blocked:` void (contracts.md's "Stale-declaration voiding" bullet, second half: "A `blocked:` is voided only when the session is observed actively GENERATING... past the same grace") is explicitly UNCHANGED and MUST be called out in the revised text as orthogonal to this supersession -- it is a distinct mechanism that happens to have shared prose with the ready-void in the current ratified text; the revision MUST sever that shared prose into two independently-stated rules rather than one bullet covering both.

**contracts.md, "Stale-declaration voiding" bullet (~lines 39-64):** Replace the ready-void half with: a `ready` declaration exceeding a bounded maximum age (30 minutes by default; MAY be configured) since its own modification time is EXPIRED, regardless of session activity -- the daemon MUST NOT void a `ready` on activity/busy/gated observation; it remains armed and eligible to certify until either the restart interlock's own settle/busy/identity gates authorize a restart, or this maximum age is exceeded. Preserve verbatim: "Voiding [now: Expiry] clears the DECLARATION ONLY. The daemon MUST delete the state file and MUST NOT delete the round's sidecar key, MUST NOT reset its notified bands, and MUST NOT otherwise close the round" (retarget to expiry; the `blocked:` void keeps this same clearing discipline stated as its own, separate sentence). Preserve verbatim, retargeted: the ORDERED two-step rule ("the daemon MUST record the expiry instant... BEFORE deleting the state file" -- same crash-safety rationale, unchanged) and "Only the `ready` expiry raises the certification floor" (the `blocked:` void still MUST NOT raise it, unchanged).

**contracts.md, "The restart interlock" (~lines 108-202):** Retarget every "void"/"voided" occurrence to "expiry"/"expired": precondition 1's floor definition ("where a `ready` declaration has since been [expired], the floor is the LATER of that stamp and the instant of the most recent such [expiry]"); the non-monotonic-clock defense paragraph (163-172, preserved verbatim with terminology retargeted -- this paragraph's reasoning is UNCHANGED and, if anything, stronger under expiry, since the expiry instant is deterministically `declaration mtime + max age` rather than depending on when a busy observation happened to land); the one-declaration-never-two-kills disjunction (174-183, retargeted, unchanged reasoning); the identity-binding paragraph (185-202, retargeted, unchanged reasoning -- "Where an [expiry] is observed with a live identity DIFFERING from the round-open identity...").

**contracts.md, round sidecar contract (~lines 346-363):** Retarget "VOID INSTANT" to "EXPIRY INSTANT" and "the void instant MAY be raised repeatedly within the round" to "the expiry instant MAY be raised repeatedly within the round" (unchanged: it can only be raised once per declaration in practice, but the field's write discipline allows re-raising if a later declaration in the same round also expires). "a voided declaration leaves the key in place" becomes "an expired declaration leaves the key in place."

**contracts.md, attention membership (~lines 453-459):** Retarget the three void-grounded members: "a track whose state-file delete AND floor record BOTH failed in the same observation" (unchanged reasoning, now about expiry's own fail-soft pair); "a track whose round record is MALFORMED under the certification-floor rules" (unchanged, not void-specific); "a track whose [expiry] was observed under a live identity DIFFERING from its round-open identity" (retargeted).

**spec.md ~lines 185-188:** "No other daemon behavior -- voiding a declaration included -- MAY delete such a round's durable record" becomes "...expiring a declaration included...".

**spec.md ~lines 204-207:** "A round carries a CERTIFICATION FLOOR, and that floor MAY rise within the round -- when a declaration is voided" becomes "...when a declaration expires...".

**spec.md ~line 215:** "the stamp, the notified bands, any recorded void floor, and the round-open identity" becomes "...any recorded expiry floor...".

**spec.md ~line 233:** "a standing declaration that can neither certify nor be voided holds its round open indefinitely" becomes "...can neither certify nor expire..." -- NOTE for the reviewer: confirm this remains reachable; a round-less standing declaration (spec.md 784-792) is explicitly OUT of the round-scoped expiry mechanism (see below), so this sentence describes a declaration that IS in a round but whose floor/identity state is malformed, which independently cannot expire in the durable-record sense even though its underlying file could still age past the max age -- if this is confusing as written, the reviewer MAY prefer to drop "nor be voided"/"nor expire" entirely here rather than retarget it; flagging as an open question rather than resolved unilaterally.

**spec.md ~lines 264-280 (the notice paragraph, superseding the first draft's version):** Replace with: A `ready` declaration is never voided by intervening session activity; the restart branch's own settle/busy/identity gates already prevent killing mid-work. A declaration remains armed until either a verified settled-idle observation authorizes the restart, or it EXPIRES by exceeding a bounded maximum age (30 minutes by default), whichever comes first. Expiry, like the restart, is a round-closing-adjacent event that raises the certification floor and deletes the state file (per contracts.md "Stale-declaration voiding" and "The restart interlock") -- an expired declaration can neither certify a restart nor persist to be mistaken for a live one. When a `ready` declaration expires inside a DELIVERED round, the daemon MUST send the session one EXPIRY-NOTICE: a message stating that its ready declaration expired without a verified settled-idle observation, restating the exact state-file path and the three values it may write, and restating that a restart requires a fresh ready. The expiry-notice is subject to the complete guarded-paste predicate that governs a wrap-up, with one difference: its trigger is the expiry itself, not the below-threshold context trigger, so it MAY fire at any known context while its round remains open -- though a round closed as recovered before the notice lands sends no notice, the fresh round's own wrap-up re-teaching the protocol instead. The notice is sent at most ONCE per round however many expiries the round accumulates, and that bound is DURABLE alongside the round's notified bands, so a daemon restart never re-sends a notice already sent. The expiry-notice is a bounded companion to the escalation, not a band, and it MUST NOT re-open, re-fire, or reset any notified band. A failed expiry-notice paste MUST NOT un-open the round and MAY be retried on a later observation within the same round's single-notice bound. The expiry-notice authorizes nothing. This mechanism is scoped to a DELIVERED round only, mirroring the old void-notice; a round-less standing declaration (spec.md "A declaration standing on a track that has NEVER been in a round") has no round to expire within and remains governed entirely by that existing, separate, continuously-surfaced rule -- it is neither expired nor notice-eligible under this paragraph.

**spec.md ~lines 309-312:** Replace "A session that declares `ready` and then resumes work has its now-false declaration voided rather than honored later; a declaration young enough to be the declaring turn's own busy tail survives (per contracts.md 'The state file')" with: "A session that declares `ready` and then resumes work -- narrates further, emits more turns, streams stop-hook output -- keeps its declaration armed; the restart branch's settle/busy gates prevent a kill mid-work without requiring any activity-based clearing. The declaration is cleared only by a successful restart or by exceeding its bounded maximum age (per contracts.md 'Stale-declaration voiding')."

**spec.md ~line 378:** "the once-per-round void-notice of §'The escalating wrap-up'" becomes "the once-per-round expiry-notice of §'The escalating wrap-up'".

**spec.md ~lines 784-792 (unchanged in substance, terminology only):** "no injection stamp and no recorded void" becomes "no injection stamp and no recorded expiry".

**scenarios.md ~lines 182-194, retarget (per the independent reviewer's own recommendation over outright deletion):** Replace 'A ready declaration is voided when its session resumes work' with: Given a session that declared ready and then went busy again / When the daemon next observes the track / Then the declaration remains armed and no restart occurs while the pane is not verified settled-idle / And the declaration is not cleared, expired, or otherwise altered by the activity / And the round's durable record and already-notified escalation bands are unaffected.

**scenarios.md ~lines 196-207, retarget (not retire, per the independent review's finding that the case is not vacuous -- a round can accumulate multiple expiries across successive fresh declarations):** '## Scenario: Repeated expiries never re-send an already-notified band' -- Given a session that repeatedly declares ready and each declaration ages past the maximum without a settled-idle observation / When each declaration expires in turn within the same open round / Then no escalation band already notified in the open round is sent again / And the round's durable record and notified bands survive every expiry / And at most one expiry-notice is sent within the round however many declarations expire.

**scenarios.md ~lines 249-263:** Replace 'A voided ready declaration is answered with one durable bounded void-notice' with '## Scenario: An expired ready declaration is answered with one durable bounded expiry-notice' -- Given a delivered round in which a session's ready declaration expired / When the daemon next completes an observation whose guarded-paste predicate passes / Then the session receives one expiry-notice naming the state-file path and the fresh-ready requirement / And a second expiry within the same round sends no second notice, even across a daemon restart / And no notified escalation band is re-sent and no restart is authorized.

**scenarios.md ~line 293:** "the track has neither an injection stamp nor a recorded void" becomes "...nor a recorded expiry".

**scenarios.md ~lines 303-315:** Replace 'A ready declaration written after a void certifies without a new round' with '## Scenario: A ready declaration written after an expiry certifies without a new round' -- Given a session whose earlier ready declaration expired / And the session later writes ready again, after the instant of that expiry / And the pane is idle, settled, and positively identified as this track's session / When the daemon evaluates the restart interlock / Then the interlock passes and the session is restarted / And no new wrap-up was required to authorize it.

**scenarios.md ~lines 317-325:** Replace 'A voided declaration never certifies against its own void' with '## Scenario: An expired declaration never certifies against its own expiry' -- Given a ready declaration that expired past the maximum age / And the state file still carried that same declaration unchanged before expiry deleted it / When the daemon evaluates the restart interlock immediately before expiry deletes the file / Then the interlock fails, because the declaration predates the instant of its own expiry.

**scenarios.md ~lines 327-335:** "no injection stamp and no recorded void" becomes "...and no recorded expiry" (case is otherwise unaffected -- unrelated to activity, about a track that was never in a round at all).

**scenarios.md ~lines 337-347:** Replace 'A successor session never certifies against its predecessor's floor' framing 'voided' with 'expired': Given a track whose declaration expired, leaving a certification floor / And the supervised session at that pane was replaced out of band after that expiry / When the successor session writes ready after that floor / Then the interlock holds, because the identity at the pane differs from the round-open identity / And the track is surfaced to the operator rather than restarted.

**scenarios.md ~lines 349-361:** Replace 'A session replaced before the void never inherits a certifiable floor' with '## Scenario: A session replaced before the expiry never inherits a certifiable floor' -- Given a round opened for a session that received the wrap-up / And that session was replaced out of band before its declaration expired / When the daemon expires the inherited declaration and the successor later writes ready / Then no certifiable floor was established by that expiry / And the interlock holds, because the identity at the pane differs from the round-open identity / And the successor is surfaced rather than restarted, having received no wrap-up.
