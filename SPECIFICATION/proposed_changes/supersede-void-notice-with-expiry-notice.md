## Proposal: Retarget the ready-void mechanism to max-age expiry across spec.md, scenarios.md, and contracts.md (supersedes and replaces proposed_changes/supersede-void-notice-with-expiry-notice.md)

### Target specification files

- SPECIFICATION/spec.md
- SPECIFICATION/scenarios.md
- SPECIFICATION/contracts.md
- SPECIFICATION/non-functional-requirements.md

### Summary

Third revision of this proposal. Round 1 (an independent Fable-model adversarial review) returned the original draft NOT READY with four blocking findings, all addressed in round 2's revision; round 2's own independent review (a fresh instance of the same reviewer) verified all four fixed, then found two NEW blocking issues plus two should-fix items in the round-2 draft, which this revision addresses. Scope, updated: (1) contracts.md's ready-void mechanism -- the ordered two-step death, certification floor, sidecar field, attention members -- is retargeted onto max-age expiry; (2) every other spec.md void-adjacent location is swept; (3) expiry explicitly DELETES the state file and RAISES the certification floor; (4) all nine affected scenarios.md entries are retargeted; (5) the 30-minute default is named explicitly; (6) the `blocked:` void is explicitly severed as unchanged and now restates its own two-minute grace independently (round 2's severing left it referencing a deleted antecedent -- round-3 fix); (7) NEW: the restart interlock is given an explicit age-based backstop precondition so a `ready` declaration cannot certify during the window between exceeding its maximum age and that expiry being durably recorded, independent of processing order within an observation -- round 2's revision stated expiry raises the floor but never closed this timing gap, which a fresh independent review caught as still allowing a stale declaration to certify; (8) the expiry instant is stated to be the deterministic value `declaration mtime + maximum age`, not a wall-clock reading at processing time, resolving an ambiguity the preserved non-monotonic-clock paragraph left open; (9) SPECIFICATION/non-functional-requirements.md's "voiding graces" beside-test reference is added as a fourth target and retargeted.

### Motivation

Epic overseer-xkrwm3 (resume-submit-integrity), fronts 4-6, maintainer-directed 2026-08-16, ruling relayed the same day: work-item overseer-xawpyl's arm-until-idle redesign (PR #1001) retires activity-based ready-voiding to fix a confabulation trap, and the daemon's obligation to notify the session when its declaration dies transfers from the retired activity-void trigger to the new max-age-expiry trigger.

Round 1 review found: contracts.md untargeted despite holding the actual voiding mechanism; four other spec.md contradiction sites; no statement of whether expiry deletes the file/raises the floor (making the notice undeliverable and weakening the stale-certification guard); an incomplete scenario sweep (2 of ~9). Round 2's revision fixed all four, verified independently by a second reviewer instance against the ratified text directly (not just the proposal's own claims).

That same round-2 review then found two further blocking issues in the round-2 draft, both addressed here: (B1) an "aged-but-unrecorded" window -- because expiry (unlike the old busy-triggered void) can be satisfied by the SAME observation that also satisfies the restart interlock's idle/settle gates, a declaration that has exceeded its maximum age but whose expiry has not yet been durably recorded could, absent an explicit rule, still pass the interlock's precondition 3 (mtime strictly newer than the recorded floor) in that single observation -- the old void/certify exclusivity (busy triggers voiding, idle triggers certification) that made the ordering question moot does not carry over to a deterministic age-based trigger. Fixed here by adding an explicit interlock precondition that gates on the declaration's own age directly, independent of recorded state, as a fail-closed backstop to the recorded-floor mechanism. (B2) An incomplete terminology sweep left contracts.md's "voiding grace" cross-reference (the constant governing how much later a recorded instant may lag the modification time it is derived from) unretargeted -- it is not a grace under expiry, it IS the maximum age -- and left the severed `blocked:` void rule referencing "the same grace" with no antecedent once the shared bullet was split. Both fixed by name here.

Two should-fix items from round 2 are also addressed: SPECIFICATION/non-functional-requirements.md's beside-test pin on "the voiding graces" (a fourth spec file neither round 1 nor round 2 caught) is retargeted; and spec.md line 233's "can neither certify nor be voided" retarget to "nor expire" is confirmed correct as originally drafted (round 2 the residual narrows under expiry -- an idle uncertifiable declaration now ages out at 30 minutes rather than holding indefinitely -- but the clause remains accurate for the residual cases: persistent fail-soft delete/record failures, a malformed round record, and an identity-differing expiry).

### Proposed Changes

GENERAL RENAME, applied throughout spec.md, scenarios.md, and contracts.md wherever the text below does not give more specific replacement wording: `void`/`voided`/`voiding` (in the READY-declaration sense only -- NOT the separate, unchanged `blocked:` void) becomes `expire`/`expired`/`expiry`; `void instant` becomes `expiry instant`; `void-notice` becomes `expiry-notice`; `VOID INSTANT` (the sidecar field name) becomes `EXPIRY INSTANT`; `voiding grace` (the constant governing how much a recorded instant may lag the modification time it derives from) becomes `maximum age` -- NOT "expiry grace"; there is no grace on the ready side under this model, only the fixed maximum age. The `blocked:` void (contracts.md's "Stale-declaration voiding" bullet, second half) is explicitly UNCHANGED and orthogonal to this supersession, but the revision MUST sever the shared bullet into two FULLY INDEPENDENT statements, each restating its own constant by name -- do NOT leave the severed `blocked:` rule referencing "the same grace" or any other cross-reference into the retargeted ready-side text; the `blocked:` void keeps its existing two-minute grace, stated explicitly in its own sentence.

**contracts.md, "Stale-declaration voiding" bullet (~lines 39-64), severed into two independent statements:** (a) Ready-side, retargeted: a `ready` declaration exceeding a bounded maximum age (30 minutes by default; MAY be configured) since its own modification time is EXPIRED, regardless of session activity -- the daemon MUST NOT void a `ready` on activity/busy/gated observation; it remains armed and eligible to certify until either the restart interlock's own settle/busy/identity gates authorize a restart, or this maximum age is exceeded. The expiry instant is the DETERMINISTIC value `declaration modification time + maximum age`, computed from the declaration's own recorded mtime -- never a separate wall-clock reading taken at the moment of processing. (b) `Blocked:`-side, unchanged in effect but now self-contained: restate verbatim, in full, without cross-referencing the ready-side text: "A `blocked:` declaration is voided only when the session is observed actively GENERATING (incompatible with waiting for an answer) past its own two-minute grace -- a session merely running a background command at its prompt keeps its declaration, however old, and an idle blocked session is never touched." Preserve verbatim, retargeted to the ready side only: "[Expiry] clears the DECLARATION ONLY. The daemon MUST delete the state file and MUST NOT delete the round's sidecar key, MUST NOT reset its notified bands, and MUST NOT otherwise close the round" -- restate this clearing discipline a second time, independently, for the `blocked:` void, since the two mechanisms no longer share one bullet. Preserve verbatim, retargeted: the ORDERED two-step rule ("the daemon MUST record the expiry instant... BEFORE deleting the state file" -- same crash-safety rationale, unchanged) and "Only the `ready` expiry raises the certification floor" (the `blocked:` void still MUST NOT raise it, unchanged).

**contracts.md, "The restart interlock" (~lines 108-202), retargeted, PLUS one new precondition clause (fixes the aged-but-unrecorded window a round-2 independent review found):** Retarget every "void"/"voided" occurrence to "expiry"/"expired": precondition 1's floor definition ("where a `ready` declaration has since been [expired], the floor is the LATER of that stamp and the instant of the most recent such [expiry]"); the non-monotonic-clock defense paragraph (163-172, preserved verbatim with terminology retargeted, PLUS the deterministic-instant clarification from the bullet above -- since the expiry instant is `mtime + maximum age` rather than a separate clock reading, the "backward clock step" hazard this paragraph defends against cannot arise on the expiry side the way it could on the old void side; state this explicitly rather than leaving the paragraph's premise ambiguous about which side it still applies to; the paragraph's conclusion -- exclusion is a property of the RECORDED floor, not of wall-clock time -- remains the correct DURABLE mechanism and is unchanged, but see the new precondition below for why recording is not the ONLY thing that must gate certification); the one-declaration-never-two-kills disjunction (174-183, retargeted, unchanged reasoning); the identity-binding paragraph (185-202, retargeted, unchanged reasoning -- "Where an [expiry] is observed with a live identity DIFFERING from the round-open identity...").

ADD a new sentence to precondition 3 (the modification-time check): "A `ready` declaration whose own age -- the current instant minus its modification time -- exceeds the maximum age MUST NOT certify under this precondition, whether or not its expiry has yet been durably recorded in the round's sidecar. This is a fail-closed backstop independent of the recorded-floor mechanism above: because expiry (unlike the retired activity-void) is satisfiable by the SAME observation that also satisfies this interlock's other preconditions (an idle, settled, correctly-identified pane), a declaration could otherwise certify in the single observation between exceeding its maximum age and that expiry being recorded. The recorded floor remains the DURABLE record of expiry across restarts and observations; this age check is the point-in-time defense against the gap before that record exists." Note for the implementer (out of scope for this proposal's own ratification, but material to the corresponding code amendment on PR #1001): this backstop means the interlock evaluation itself must be able to read a declaration's own age directly, not only the round's recorded floor -- both checks apply, and either failing MUST fail the precondition.

**contracts.md, round sidecar contract (~lines 346-363):** Retarget "VOID INSTANT" to "EXPIRY INSTANT" and "the void instant MAY be raised repeatedly within the round" to "the expiry instant MAY be raised repeatedly within the round" (unchanged: it can only be raised once per declaration in practice, but the field's write discipline allows re-raising if a later declaration in the same round also expires). "a voided declaration leaves the key in place" becomes "an expired declaration leaves the key in place."

**contracts.md, attention membership (~lines 453-459):** Retarget the three void-grounded members: "a track whose state-file delete AND floor record BOTH failed in the same observation" (unchanged reasoning, now about expiry's own fail-soft pair); "a track whose round record is MALFORMED under the certification-floor rules" (unchanged, not void-specific); "a track whose [expiry] was observed under a live identity DIFFERING from its round-open identity" (retargeted).

**spec.md ~lines 185-188:** "No other daemon behavior -- voiding a declaration included -- MAY delete such a round's durable record" becomes "...expiring a declaration included...".

**spec.md ~lines 204-207:** "A round carries a CERTIFICATION FLOOR, and that floor MAY rise within the round -- when a declaration is voided" becomes "...when a declaration expires...".

**spec.md ~line 215:** "the stamp, the notified bands, any recorded void floor, and the round-open identity" becomes "...any recorded expiry floor...".

**spec.md ~line 233:** "a standing declaration that can neither certify nor be voided holds its round open indefinitely" becomes "...can neither certify nor expire..." -- NOTE for the reviewer: confirm this remains reachable; a round-less standing declaration (spec.md 784-792) is explicitly OUT of the round-scoped expiry mechanism (see below), so this sentence describes a declaration that IS in a round but whose floor/identity state is malformed, which independently cannot expire in the durable-record sense even though its underlying file could still age past the max age -- if this is confusing as written, the reviewer MAY prefer to drop "nor be voided"/"nor expire" entirely here rather than retarget it; flagging as an open question rather than resolved unilaterally.

**spec.md ~lines 264-280 (the notice paragraph, superseding the first draft's version):** Replace with: A `ready` declaration is never voided by intervening session activity; the restart branch's own settle/busy/identity gates already prevent killing mid-work. A declaration remains armed until either a verified settled-idle observation authorizes the restart, or it EXPIRES by exceeding a bounded maximum age (30 minutes by default), whichever comes first. Unlike the restart, expiry does NOT close the round -- it raises the certification floor and deletes the state file (per contracts.md "Stale-declaration voiding" and "The restart interlock") while leaving the round's durable record, notified bands, and open status untouched -- an expired declaration can neither certify a restart nor persist to be mistaken for a live one. When a `ready` declaration expires inside a DELIVERED round, the daemon MUST send the session one EXPIRY-NOTICE: a message stating that its ready declaration expired without a verified settled-idle observation, restating the exact state-file path and the three values it may write, and restating that a restart requires a fresh ready. The expiry-notice is subject to the complete guarded-paste predicate that governs a wrap-up, with one difference: its trigger is the expiry itself, not the below-threshold context trigger, so it MAY fire at any known context while its round remains open -- though a round closed as recovered before the notice lands sends no notice, the fresh round's own wrap-up re-teaching the protocol instead. The notice is sent at most ONCE per round however many expiries the round accumulates, and that bound is DURABLE alongside the round's notified bands, so a daemon restart never re-sends a notice already sent. The expiry-notice is a bounded companion to the escalation, not a band, and it MUST NOT re-open, re-fire, or reset any notified band. A failed expiry-notice paste MUST NOT un-open the round and MAY be retried on a later observation within the same round's single-notice bound. The expiry-notice authorizes nothing. This mechanism is scoped to a DELIVERED round only, mirroring the old void-notice; a round-less standing declaration (spec.md "A declaration standing on a track that has NEVER been in a round") has no round to expire within and remains governed entirely by that existing, separate, continuously-surfaced rule -- it is neither expired nor notice-eligible under this paragraph.

**spec.md ~lines 309-312:** Replace "A session that declares `ready` and then resumes work has its now-false declaration voided rather than honored later; a declaration young enough to be the declaring turn's own busy tail survives (per contracts.md 'The state file')" with: "A session that declares `ready` and then resumes work -- narrates further, emits more turns, streams stop-hook output -- keeps its declaration armed; the restart branch's settle/busy gates prevent a kill mid-work without requiring any activity-based clearing. The declaration is cleared only by a successful restart or by exceeding its bounded maximum age (per contracts.md 'Stale-declaration voiding')."

**spec.md ~line 378:** "the once-per-round void-notice of §'The escalating wrap-up'" becomes "the once-per-round expiry-notice of §'The escalating wrap-up'".

**spec.md ~lines 784-792 (unchanged in substance, terminology only):** "no injection stamp and no recorded void" becomes "no injection stamp and no recorded expiry".

**scenarios.md ~lines 182-194, retarget (per the independent reviewer's own recommendation over outright deletion):** Replace 'A ready declaration is voided when its session resumes work' with '## Scenario: A ready declaration remains armed when its session resumes work' -- Given a session that declared ready and then went busy again / When the daemon next observes the track / Then the declaration remains armed and no restart occurs while the pane is not verified settled-idle / And the declaration is not cleared, expired, or otherwise altered by the activity / And the round's durable record and already-notified escalation bands are unaffected.

**scenarios.md ~lines 196-207, retarget (not retire, per the independent review's finding that the case is not vacuous -- a round can accumulate multiple expiries across successive fresh declarations):** '## Scenario: Repeated expiries never re-send an already-notified band' -- Given a session that repeatedly declares ready and each declaration ages past the maximum without a settled-idle observation / When each declaration expires in turn within the same open round / Then no escalation band already notified in the open round is sent again / And the round's durable record and notified bands survive every expiry / And at most one expiry-notice is sent within the round however many declarations expire.

**scenarios.md ~lines 249-263:** Replace 'A voided ready declaration is answered with one durable bounded void-notice' with '## Scenario: An expired ready declaration is answered with one durable bounded expiry-notice' -- Given a delivered round in which a session's ready declaration expired / When the daemon next completes an observation whose guarded-paste predicate passes / Then the session receives one expiry-notice naming the state-file path and the fresh-ready requirement / And a second expiry within the same round sends no second notice, even across a daemon restart / And no notified escalation band is re-sent and no restart is authorized.

**scenarios.md ~line 293:** "the track has neither an injection stamp nor a recorded void" becomes "...nor a recorded expiry".

**scenarios.md ~lines 303-315:** Replace 'A ready declaration written after a void certifies without a new round' with '## Scenario: A ready declaration written after an expiry certifies without a new round' -- Given a session whose earlier ready declaration expired / And the session later writes ready again, after the instant of that expiry / And the pane is idle, settled, and positively identified as this track's session / When the daemon evaluates the restart interlock / Then the interlock passes and the session is restarted / And no new wrap-up was required to authorize it.

**scenarios.md ~lines 317-325:** Replace 'A voided declaration never certifies against its own void' with '## Scenario: An expired declaration never certifies against its own expiry' -- Given a ready declaration that expired past the maximum age / And the state file still carried that same declaration unchanged before expiry deleted it / When the daemon evaluates the restart interlock immediately before expiry deletes the file / Then the interlock fails, because the declaration predates the instant of its own expiry.

**scenarios.md ~lines 327-335:** "no injection stamp and no recorded void" becomes "...and no recorded expiry" (case is otherwise unaffected -- unrelated to activity, about a track that was never in a round at all).

**scenarios.md ~lines 337-347:** Replace 'A successor session never certifies against its predecessor's floor' framing 'voided' with 'expired': Given a track whose declaration expired, leaving a certification floor / And the supervised session at that pane was replaced out of band after that expiry / When the successor session writes ready after that floor / Then the interlock holds, because the identity at the pane differs from the round-open identity / And the track is surfaced to the operator rather than restarted.

**scenarios.md ~lines 349-361:** Replace 'A session replaced before the void never inherits a certifiable floor' with '## Scenario: A session replaced before the expiry never inherits a certifiable floor' -- Given a round opened for a session that received the wrap-up / And that session was replaced out of band before its declaration expired / When the daemon expires the inherited declaration and the successor later writes ready / Then no certifiable floor was established by that expiry / And the interlock holds, because the identity at the pane differs from the round-open identity / And the successor is surfaced rather than restarted, having received no wrap-up.

**SPECIFICATION/non-functional-requirements.md ~line 20:** "Every protocol behavior in this specification -- the interlock, the voiding graces, the band escalation, the nudge lifecycle, the submit retry -- is pinned by a deterministic beside-test" becomes "...the interlock, the expiry maximum age and the blocked-void grace, the band escalation...".
